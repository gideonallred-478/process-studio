# Process Studio

## Short description

Record how you do a task. Process Studio turns the explanation into a transcript, summary, step-by-step procedure, action items and a workflow blueprint you can review and reuse.

## Problem

A walkthrough often leaves the viewer with another job: replay the video, take notes, identify exceptions and translate the explanation into repeatable work. Knowledge stays inside a recording instead of becoming a usable procedure.

## Product

Screen and camera recording with a movable circular camera; automatic upload and processing; playback and an editable transcript; a dedicated result page; source-backed procedure steps and follow-ups; JSON export; and explicit snapshot sharing with revocation.

## Improvement over the recording-only workflow

The explanation becomes a working procedure. Each step keeps a source quote and distinguishes three questions: can the task repeat automatically, does it need access to another app, and does somebody need to approve it? The blueprint shows missing dependencies before someone tries to automate the work.

This describes our product focus, not a claim that Loom lacks AI features. Process Studio does not connect or execute integrations automatically.

## Demo use case

A customer inquiry arrives. Log the requested details, prepare a routine response, route complaints or refunds to the manager, and follow up after two business days. Repeatable data entry and human judgment remain distinct.
