## Suggested narration

“When a customer inquiry arrives, open it in the CRM and check the requested service. Copy the customer's name, email and requested date into the follow-up sheet. For a routine question, choose the matching response template and personalize the opening. If the message mentions a complaint or refund, flag it for the manager instead. Send the approved reply from the shared inbox and add a CRM note. Follow up after two business days if there is no reply. Review the sheet each Friday for missed inquiries.”
