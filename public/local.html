Process Studio

Record locally. Share usable work.

The Windows x64 edition runs Whisper transcription and Qwen reasoning on your computer. No API key is required. You can also connect your own eligible ChatGPT plan locally.

Download local Studio

  1. Extract the complete ZIP to a folder you can write to.
  2. Run Install-Studio.ps1. The first setup downloads several GB of verified runtimes and models. CPU support is included; speed depends on your computer.
  3. Run Start-Studio.ps1 and open http://127.0.0.1:4182/ in Edge or Chrome.
  4. In Settings, connect Online sharing. Review your transcript and every process decision, then choose Publish online on the result page.

Nothing uploads automatically. Video is optional. The movable desktop camera requires this Windows edition; the hosted browser uses its supported preview instead.

Current limits: recordings under six minutes for local transcription, up to 25 MB imports, and 12,000 characters for AI analysis. Longer saved sources are retained; split them before generation.
